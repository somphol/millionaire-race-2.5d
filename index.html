The Playbook

How to win

🏆 Victory

First player to reach $1,000,000 total net worth at the end of a turn wins instantly. If nobody does, the highest net worth after 30 rounds wins.

💵 Economy

Everyone starts with $100,000. Net worth = cash + the buy value of every asset you own. Passing or landing on GO pays $20,000 plus a 10% dividend on owned stocks.

🏙️ Assets & Rent

Land on an unowned asset to buy it or pass. Land on a rival's asset and pay rent from cash. If cash is short, assets auto-mortgage at 50% of purchase value.

💀 Bankruptcy

If you cannot cover a debt and have no assets left to mortgage, you are eliminated and your assets return to the bank.

🤖 AI Rivals

Bots buy an asset when remaining cash stays above $20,000, and pay the $10,000 audit-lockout exit fee whenever their cash is above $30,000.

🔒 Lockout

Tile 18 sends you to the Luxury Club and costs your next turn — unless you pay the $10,000 exit fee. Tax loophole cards cancel your next tax bill.

Tip: press R to roll, Esc to close dialogs.
